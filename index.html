Privacy Policy — SELECT Chatbot (WhatsApp)

Last updated: July 30, 2026

This privacy policy explains how AMDB Co., Ltd. ("we", "our") collects, uses, and protects the information of people who interact with our automated assistant on WhatsApp (the "Service").

1. Information We Collect

As part of using the Service, we collect:

  • Your WhatsApp phone number, used to identify your conversation and respond to you.
  • The content of messages exchanged with the bot, in order to process your requests and improve our responses.

We do not collect any other personal data (no precise location, no payment data) through this Service.

2. How We Use Your Information

The information collected is used solely to:

  • Respond to your messages and provide the requested service;
  • Ensure the proper functioning, support, and improvement of the bot;
  • Compile anonymized internal statistics on Service usage.

We do not sell or rent your data to third parties.

3. Sharing of Information

Your data may be shared with:

  • Meta Platforms, Inc., as the provider of the WhatsApp Business API infrastructure, as part of the normal exchange of messages;
  • Technical service providers (hosting, messaging) strictly necessary for the operation of the Service, who are bound by confidentiality obligations.

4. Data Retention

Messages and phone numbers are retained for as long as necessary for the proper functioning of the Service and may be deleted upon request (see Section 6).

5. Security

We implement reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your data against unauthorized access, loss, or disclosure.

6. Your Rights

You may, at any time:

  • Request access to the data we hold about you;
  • Request its deletion;
  • Object to its processing, or withdraw your consent, by simply ceasing to use the Service or by contacting us.

7. Contact

For any question regarding this policy or your data, please contact us at: contact@select.guide

8. Changes

This policy may be updated periodically. The date of the last update appears at the top of this document.
